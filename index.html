Upload your workbook and Aparajitha Simpliance will identify the relevant Region and Zone fields, categorise records into North, South, East and West, and preserve the complete original information for inspection. You can then add records, update details and export the revised workbook.

What this version protects

  • Original column names are preserved.
  • Blank/formatted-only rows are ignored.
  • North/East conflicts are resolved using Zone.
  • Every record gets an individual Info button.
  • Exports contain all original fields for that category.
  • Unclear records are not silently forced into a region.

The two files are processed locally in your browser. Records are combined, duplicate field names are aligned where possible, and the existing region classification logic is retained.

For sensitive business data, the browser-only design is preferable to uploading workbook contents to a third-party server. The app still runs in the user's browser, so browser-level privacy controls remain relevant.
